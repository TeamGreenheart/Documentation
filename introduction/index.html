1Introduction

Welcome to the Greenheart website manual. It covers all three Greenheart websites. They share a theme and an editor, but each site has its own content types and blocks.

The Three Sites

PreviewSiteUsed for
Exchange
greenheartexchange.org		Inbound cultural exchange. Greenheart sponsors visas for international participants coming to the US: high school exchange, professional development (Teach USA), and intern & trainee (CAP) programs.
International
greenheart.org		The organization's main site: press, events and careers
Travel
greenhearttravel.org		Outbound cultural exchange for US participants going abroad: high school abroad, language camps, a job board for teaching English abroad, and other programs.

Using This Manual

The manual is organized by task. Instructions such as Adding a Page are written once, because the editor works the same on every site. Anything that differs by site has a badge:

Exchange International Travel All sites

Only work on one site?

Use the Showing menu at the top of the page to pick your site. Content types, blocks and features from the other sites will be hidden. The setting is remembered, and you can share a link to a filtered view (for example ?site=travel).

Getting Access

Each site has its own login, so you need a separate account for each site you edit. Sign in with your username or email address on the site's login page:

SiteLogin page
Exchangegreenheartexchange.org/wp-login.php
Internationalgreenheart.org/wp-login.php
Travelgreenhearttravel.org/wp-login.php

Need an account, or access to another site? Contact the site administrator through the Web Issue Form. Say which site or sites you need, and what you'll be working on.

Tip

Bookmark the login page for each site you edit.

Resetting Your Password

Each site has its own password, so a reset only changes the password for that site.

  1. Go to the site's login page and click Lost your password? below the login form.
  2. Enter your username or the email address on your account, then click Get New Password.
  3. Check your email for a message with a password reset link. If it doesn't arrive within a few minutes, check your spam or junk folder.
  4. Click the link in the email and enter a new password. Use a strong password: WordPress shows a strength meter as you type.
  5. Click Save Password, then sign in with your new password.
The reset link expires

Reset links can only be used once and expire after 24 hours. If yours has expired, start again from step 1.

If the reset email never arrives, or your account is locked, contact the site administrator through the Web Issue Form.

User Roles

What you can do on a site depends on your role. Most people will have one of these two:

RoleCanCan't
Greenheart EditorSee and edit all content types: pages, posts and every custom typeChange theme settings, menus or widgets
Greenheart AdminEverything an Editor can do, plus theme settings, menus and widgetsManage redirects, security settings, or add and update plugins and themes

Anything outside your role can be requested through the Web Issue Form.
