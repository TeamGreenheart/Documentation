2Content Types

A content type is a kind of content, such as a page, a news item or a program. Each site has its own custom content types, but all of them use the standard block editor. The steps in Adding & Editing Content apply to every type.

Most custom types are in the Greenheart Content menu (heart icon) in the admin. Some have their own top-level menu item; each entry below says where to find it.

Greenheart International

News Items

Coverage of Greenheart on other websites. News items are listed on the /press/ page together with Press Releases. Set each item's News Source to In the News in the sidebar. Then fill in the News Link box with the full address of the article, starting with https://. On the /press/ page, the item's card opens that article in a new tab.

For Greenheart's own announcements, add a Press Release instead.

Admin menu
Greenheart Content › Press
Web address
/press/, filtered by category at /press/{category}/
Filters
News Source, Categories, Tags
In the News items need a link to publish

See Adding News Items for the steps, what counts as a valid link, and troubleshooting.

Byline: Greenheart Staff or Guest Author

The Guest Byline box in the editor's sidebar decides who the release is credited to:

  • Greenheart Staff: turn on Attribute to Greenheart Staff. The release is credited to Greenheart Staff instead of your user account.
  • Guest Author: leave the toggle off and fill in Guest Author Name, Guest Author Title (for example "CEO, Partner Organization") and Guest Author Thumbnail (a headshot). The release then shows that person's name, title and photo in place of a WordPress author.
  • Neither: if the toggle is off and the guest name is empty, the release is credited to the author set in WordPress.

The staff toggle wins: if it's on, the guest author fields are ignored.

Linking Apply Now to Paylocity

Every career's Apply Now button must open the job's application page on Paylocity:

  1. In the sidebar, find Link an Apply Form? and choose External Link.
  2. Save the career (Save draft or Update). The External Link box only appears after saving.
  3. Paste the job's full Paylocity application address into External Link, then save again.

To change the button's wording, use Custom CTA Text?. If you leave it empty, the button says "Apply Now".
