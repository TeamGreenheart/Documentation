2Content Types

A content type is a kind of content, such as a page, a news item or a program. Each site has its own custom content types, but all of them use the standard block editor. The steps in Adding & Editing Content apply to every type.

Most custom types are in the Greenheart Content menu (heart icon) in the admin. Some have their own top-level menu item; each entry below says where to find it.

Greenheart International

News Items

News coverage and Greenheart's own press releases, listed on the /press/ page. Every news item needs a News Source, set in the News Source panel in the editor's sidebar:

  • In the News: coverage of Greenheart on another website. Fill in the News Link box with the full address of the article (starting with https://). On the /press/ page, the item's card opens that article in a new tab.
  • Press Releases: Greenheart's own announcements. Write the release in the item's content; no link is needed.
Admin menu
Greenheart Content › press
Web address
/press/, filtered by category at /press/{category}/
Filters
News Source, Categories, Tags
In the News items need a link to publish

When you tick In the News, the News Link box appears and is required. Until it has a full http:// or https:// address, a warning stays at the top of the editor. You can still save a draft, but you can't publish or schedule the item. On an item that's already published, Update is locked until the link is fixed.

Press release pages are being retired

Press releases used to be pages under /press-releases/, each with a news item linking to it. Don't create new press release pages. Add a news item with the Press Releases source instead. The old pages will be removed.
